# Boldest Original Concept — $25,000

**Judges reward:** "The idea that couldn't exist anywhere else. A concept
whose core loop depends on presence, spatial depth, real-world scale, or
eyes and hands. Judged on originality and on whether the experience would
fall apart on any other platform."

## Why Loci wins it

**The claim: thinking-in-3D and the memory palace both fall apart on a flat
screen — Loci is unbuildable without VR.**

- A notes app can *list* connections; it cannot make you **stand inside** the
  structure of your own mind, reach out, and move a thought.
- Spaced repetition exists (Anki); a memory palace that **persists, grows,
  and can be rewound** exists nowhere — Rewind (scrubbing your own mental
  history through physical space) is the beat no other platform can even
  imitate.
- The snap — scattered thoughts resolving into a constellation *around you* —
  uses presence as the medium, not the garnish.
- The method of loci is a 2,000-year-old memory technique that has always
  required an imagined *place*; VR is the first medium where the place is
  real. Loci closes a two-millennium loop.

**No code needed or wanted here** — this award is won in the pitch and the
demo. Adding mechanics to chase "boldness" this close to submission repeats
the exact scope risk the project's own Risks doc warns about.

## The flow sequence (pure pitch — weave through the whole video)

1. **Open on the claim**, over the empty void: "Two thousand years ago,
   orators built memory palaces in their minds. VR is the first medium that
   can build the palace *for* you."
2. **The snap** — "your thoughts, given a place."
3. **The grab** — "understanding is physical: you *move* what you mean."
4. **Rewind** — "and no flat screen can scrub back through your mind's
   history. This is the part that falls apart on a monitor — which is the
   point."
5. **Close on the greeting line** from a return visit: "the palace is still
   here. So is the habit."
