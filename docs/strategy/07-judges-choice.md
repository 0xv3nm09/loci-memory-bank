# 7. Judge's Choice

> **Eligibility:** the wildcard award — no fixed brief; judges reward the
> entry they can't stop thinking about.

## The claim in one line

Loci's wildcard angle is the **combination**: genuine cognitive science,
ethical AI with visible honesty, zero-asset craft, and a safety net most
productivity apps would never build.

## What the judges reward

Whatever they loved. Loci's pitch gives every judge something memorable:

## The four memorable hooks

### 1. Real cognitive science

Method of loci (2,000-year-old memory technique) + spaced repetition — not
metaphors, but the actual mechanics of the loop. A judge who knows the
science will recognize it; one who doesn't will still feel the palace work.

### 2. Ethical, transparent AI

The gold/gray orb (which intelligence organized this?), suggestions as
offers never instructions, the distress gate running before any model call,
and flagged captures that never reach the model, the palace, or the save.
An AI product where the safety surface is a non-removable pipeline stage is
rare enough to be a hook on its own.

### 3. Zero-asset craft

Every sound is synthesized from WebAudio oscillators (snap chord, spawn
shimmer, calm tone) — zero audio files, zero licensing. All type is
canvas-rendered with a bundled OFL font. No animation library, no UI
framework — hand-built Three.js on Meta's IWSDK. Three independent code
audits; findings triaged and fixed.

### 4. The safety net as a feature

A 16-pattern distress gate on *every* capture; a 3D crisis panel with real
resources (988, findahelpline.com); `crisActive` blocking organize, recall,
capture, and rewind while open. Fail direction: toward resources. Documented
openly in [SAFETY.md](../SAFETY.md) — including the rejected ideas.

finishable* (complete loop, real polish, documented limits) rather than
chasing breadth.
