How Loci works
  1. Capture — type a thought, speak with the mic, or press a seed card to spill a whole set.
  2. Organize — touch the glowing orb: your thoughts snap into connected clusters. Sage groups them; you can always rearrange.
  3. Arrange — grab and drag any card. Your placements are never overwritten.
  4. Recall — the violet orb quizzes your weakest notes first; correct answers come back less often.
  5. Rewind — scrub time and watch your palace grow.
  6. Everything persists — export anytime from the bar below.
